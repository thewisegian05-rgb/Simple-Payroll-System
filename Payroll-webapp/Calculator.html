Payroll Get Started

Choose a Calculator

Pick one below. Each one walks you through exactly what to enter.

Gross Pay

Enter your hours and rate. Overtime hours are paid at 1.25× your hourly rate.

Tax Deduction

Enter your gross pay and state tax rate to estimate all deductions.

Net Pay

Enter your gross pay and total tax deduction to see your take-home pay.

Working Hours

Enter the time you clocked in and out to see hours worked, late entry and overtime.

Monthly Pay

Enter a typical work day and the number of days worked to project a full month's pay. Entering after 10 AM applies a 10% late penalty, and a flat 12% tax is applied to daily pay.
